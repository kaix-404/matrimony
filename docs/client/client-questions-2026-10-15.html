Decisions in detail

1. Storage residency (GAP-11)

Why it matters: Answer H3 committed the platform to India-only data residency. Cloudflare R2 (chosen 2026-10-01) offers EU, US, FedRAMP and “default” jurisdictions but no India jurisdiction; an Asia-Pacific setting is only a best-effort location hint, not a guarantee. Photographs, family details and identity documents are in scope, so this is a written commitment that needs a deliberate answer.

  • (a) Accept it — downgrade H3 to “Asia-Pacific, best-effort” and state so in the Privacy Policy.
  • (b) [Recommended] Keep an India-region bucket for photos and ID documents, use R2 for non-sensitive assets.
  • (c) Keep everything in India and drop R2.

We recommend (b) if the R2 cost saving is worthwhile, otherwise (c). We advise against (a): it resolves the conflict by weakening a commitment you have already made.

2. Discovery ordering (GAP-3)

Why it matters: Answer E2 said “newest and closest profile first”, which names two orderings. The choice changes the database index and the query, so it should be settled before the ranking is tuned. The build currently orders newest-first.

  • (a) [Recommended] Newest-registered first, using location distance only to break ties.
  • (b) A blended score combining recency and distance.
  • (c) Nearest first, newest as the tiebreak.

3. Do saved preferences filter results? (GAP-10)

Why it matters: E1 covers the case where no preference is set, but not the case where one is. If preferences filter, a narrow preference can make some profiles effectively undiscoverable. The build currently treats saved preferences as filters alongside the two-way category rule.

  • (a) [Recommended] Yes — saved preferences filter results.
  • (b) No — preferences only affect sort, never hide a profile.

4. Family photos (GAP-4)

Why it matters: D1 asked for both a single-person and a family group photo, which was not one of the offered options. Three things remain undefined, and automated photo screening behaves differently for a group photo (multiple faces).

  • Is a family photo required, or optional alongside a mandatory single photo?
  • Which photo is the cover image shown on cards?
  • Is the family photo visible before payment, or withheld like the name?
  • [Recommended] Optional; the single-person photo is the cover; not visible before payment.

5. Net-worth band boundaries (GAP-2)

Why it matters: The bands are “below ₹2 Cr” and “₹2–5 Cr”, so a value of exactly ₹2 Cr, ₹5 Cr or ₹10 Cr has no defined home. This is a data-integrity question. The build currently assigns a boundary value upward into the higher band.

  • (a) [Recommended] Confirm the current rule: exactly on a boundary goes into the higher band.
  • (b) Send exact boundary values to an admin-review bucket instead.

6. Unlock when the target profile is deleted (GAP-1)

Why it matters: Answer A4 said no refund and immediate deletion with no access. If the owner of a profile a member has just paid to contact deletes their account, the buyer loses access to a contact they paid for. At the top band that is ₹1,178.82 paid for nothing, with no appeals route (F4).

  • (a) Keep the money and grant nothing (current behaviour under A4).
  • (b) [Recommended] Issue a credit note for a future unlock — the only option that leaves the buyer whole.

7. Who may delete a member account (GAP-6)

Why it matters: F2(a) restricted category changes, pricing edits and manual unlocks to the Super Admin, but the question also asked about deleting a user and was not answered. Deletion now also triggers an archive-and-purge obligation.

  • (a) [Recommended] Super Admin only.
  • (b) Any admin holding a dedicated user:delete permission.

8. Aadhaar handling (GAP-9)

Why it matters: The platform never stores a full Aadhaar number — only the DigiLocker/UIDAI reference and a masked last-4, plus a consent record. Some verification flows appear to expect the full number; we want this confirmed as acceptable.

  • [Recommended] Confirm the app must never hold the full number (as currently built).

9. Email provider (GAP-12, remaining half)

Why it matters: SMS (MSG91) and push (Firebase) are chosen and built. Email is the last channel with no sender, so any email notification is currently recorded but not delivered. A single EMAIL_PROVIDER_API_KEY keeps the choice a configuration change.

  • Name the email provider and supply credentials (see the list below).
  • Confirm the sending domain so SPF/DKIM/DMARC can be configured.

10. Legal documents (GAP-7)

Why it matters: H1(b) puts the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service on this engagement, subject to professional review — a new line item. The app collects religion, caste, horoscope, income, family details, contact numbers and Aadhaar references.

  • [Recommended] Confirm these are in the contracted scope (and priced), not absorbed silently.

11. UI/UX design (GAP-8)

Why it matters: I1 answered “minimalist, developer team to propose designs”, which puts 18 user-facing and 14 admin screens of design in scope.

  • [Recommended] Confirm this sits inside the 30–60 day estimate and is priced accordingly.

12. Mobile platforms and store accounts

Why it matters: The mobile app (Phase 9) is the last major build. The target platforms and store ownership affect effort and release timing.

  • Android only, iOS only, or both? [Recommended: both]
  • Who owns the Google Play and Apple Developer accounts? [Recommended: the client]
  • Target Android/iOS minimum versions, if any.
